# REJECTED IDEAS — Phase 5, 2026-10-07

*Preserved per directive §40 and the lab's standing law (nothing ever fake, only real). A rejection is a finding, not a failure. Each entry: what was claimed, what killed it, the receipt. Phase-4 entries preserved verbatim; Phase-5 additions appended and marked.*

## Phase-4 entries (preserved)

1. **Error-derived affect as a controller** — Claim: valence/arousal derived from error dynamics beats a PAD dictionary. Killed by: B/K4 — PAD −0.762 > error-affect −0.787 on resource_world; error-affect saturates arousal → permanent over-exploration (NR-B-002: arousal saturates at 1.0, explore_gain=2.0 permanently, agent sits at chance). Receipt: `brothel/prototypes/architecture-b/receipts/EXP-AB-K4.json`. Keep PAD; drop the story.
2. **"Active inference" label (current EFE proxy)** — Claim: expected-free-energy action selection beats greedy on information gain. Killed by: B/C4 — greedy IG 0.0502 > AI 0.0275 > random −0.1406 (metric itself flawed — rewards exploitation; needs a better IG metric before any revival). Receipt: EXP-AB-C4.json. The label comes off.
2b. **"Active inference" label, permanent** — A sharper uncertainty-reduction probe (policy-independent, identical init) confirmed the kill: AI probe-error 0.5443 < greedy 0.7124 ≈ random 0.5497 (3 seeds). The label stays off permanently. Caveat recorded: the probe rewards concentrated practice — a lesson for future IG metric design, not a verdict change. Receipt: EXP-AB-C4B.json → NR-B-008.
3. **Precision weighting as currently implemented** — Claim: precision-from-error-statistics beats uniform weighting. Killed by: B/C2 — uniform 32.1 > precision 26.3 on noisy changing-rule (replicated by the Phase-4 coordinator's own hand: 26.300 vs 32.100); overshoot after rule flips. Receipt: EXP-AB-C2.json.
3b. **Shift-robust precision variant** — Claim: a surprise-triggered window-reset precision estimator survives shift. Killed by: B/C2B — shift_reset 23.97 < estimated 24.10 < uniform 28.47 on shift arm; 24.40 < uniform 34.00 stationary (3 seeds). Diagnostic: the surprise detector fires 82–110×/run (cannot separate rule flips from single noisy trials) and each reset reintroduces the cold-start overshoot it was meant to cure. Precision weighting rejected in BOTH tested forms; the idea is shelved. Receipt: EXP-AB-C2B.json → NR-B-007.
4. **Unwired broadcast theater** — Claim: broadcast.json / malice_port/broadcast.py integrates the system. Killed by: battery 001b — present vs import-blocked gives bit-identical trajectories, Δ=0, with a sensitive positive control (001c). The current pipeline's broadcast is decoration. (Architecture A's prototype wires broadcast as the sole path and passes K2 5/5 on fresh seeds — the pattern is keepable, the current instance is not.)
5. **attention_bias** — Claim: a bias mechanism in the attention schema. Killed by: live-root inspection — defined once at `core/attention_schema.py:46`, zero callers anywhere. Dead method; remove.
6. **Identity-directed affect-remapping guard in pad_emotion_core** — Claim: (none — it was never a cognitive claim). Killed by: directive §14 + Phase-1 audit — armed remapping of affect toward hardcoded identities inside `update()` (`_apply_devotional_guard`). REMOVE everywhere, not renamed. Dormant on the tick path (no target key) but causal where armed.
7. **Scalar-called-phi (PhiProxy)** — Claim: a number measuring integration. Killed by: Phase-2 science review (consciousness_science.md §9) — never validated against known-integration manipulations; an arbitrary scalar is not phi. Per the review: Φ is defined over a system's full cause–effect repertoire under all partitions; any single-number "integration" metric must be named for what it actually measures. Rename for what it measures or remove.
8. **"WIRED (enrichment only)" read as causal wiring** — Claim: wave-3b modules are wired into the tick. Killed by: live-root inspection — outputs are explicitly enrichment-only, *"reported, never applied."* Enrichment is telemetry, not wiring. The lesion-or-drop rule stands.
9. **Oscillatory/komplex binding story** — Claim: Kuramoto binding is a principled binding mechanism. Killed by: battery — boost non-monotone (1.458@0.5 > 1.406@0.9), phase-robust winner flips 6.3%: structured perturbation, not principled binding. EXECUTED, story unsupported. (Phase-5: per the A candidates doc they stay EXPERIMENT_AGAINST; nothing in K1–K4 needed them.)
10. **Mixture-of-experts L1 (B v1)** — Claim: MoE hierarchy. Killed by: cold-start chicken-and-egg (gating↔experts; reward predictions flat after 200 trials). Replaced by context-indexed experts (NR-B-001). The failure is preserved as the reason v2 exists.
11. **"D is a strong null"** — Claim: the lean competitor is a hard bar. Killed by: environments worker's measurements — D loses to symbolic everywhere, locks into no-op fixed points (EXP-FP-0003: 0.0000 on delayed-reward; EXP-FP-0001/0002/0004). D remains useful as a *characterized floor*, not a strong opponent. The symbolic baseline is the real bar.

## Phase-5 additions

12. **R2 stationarity detector (adaptive theta)** — Claim: lowering theta under detected stationarity resolves NR-A-004. Killed by: K6 — R2 admits ~2.7× ignitions, weakening gate selectivity; R1 resolves the freeze without touching the gate. R2 also resolves, but the selectivity cost is unacceptable. Receipt: `flesh-pits/prototypes/architecture-a/receipts/k6_stationarity_resolution.json`.
13. **"Learning is load-bearing for control on changing_rule" (K1 claim)** — Claim: B's learning machinery earns its control advantage (Phase-4 verdict SURVIVES: learn 26.2 > frozen 21.6, +4.6). Killed by: multi-seed replication 2026-10-07 — 0/5 fresh seeds hold; frozen beats learn by 0.8–10.2 on all 5 (seeds 72001–72005). Decomposition: original config reruns to exactly +4.60; changing ONLY the agent-init seed flips to −0.20; changing ONLY the env stream flips to −5.60. The +4.6 was a lucky draw on BOTH. The frozen agent's random-init weights + mu0 tracking do most of the work. Downgrade: HierarchicalGenerativeModel ADAPTIVE → INTEGRATED. (The predictor's learning itself is untouched — K2 reproduces 5/5.) Receipt: `flesh-pits/prototypes/architecture-b/receipts/repro_EXP-AB-K1.json`.
14. **The original K3 |e0| probe as a hierarchy instrument** — Claim: structured |e0| lesion delta measures hierarchical contribution (Phase-4 verdict SURVIVES, WEAK: +0.0032). Killed by: multi-seed replication — structured delta NEGATIVE on all 5 fresh seeds (−0.008…−0.028; lesion HELPS), white-noise deltas small/mixed as preregistered. The probe is seed-fragile and diluted by unpredictable channels (cue) — a bad instrument, not a disproof of hierarchy (K3B's reward-channel instrument is separate and stands). Receipt: `flesh-pits/prototypes/architecture-b/receipts/repro_EXP-AB-K3.json`.
15. **K5 temporal-prediction claim** — Claim: the model learns temporally-tracked rule structure vs bag-of-features marginals (Phase-4 verdict SURVIVES, WEAK: +0.0030). Killed by: multi-seed replication per the preregistered kill condition — shuffled-trained ≤ ordered-trained on all 5 fresh seeds (deltas −0.0002…−0.0457). Demoted. Receipt: `flesh-pits/prototypes/architecture-b/receipts/repro_EXP-AB-K5.json`.
16. **freeze_rate < 0.90 as the freeze metric (NR-A-008)** — Claim: P(action_t == action_{t-1}) < 0.90 operationalizes "the freeze is eliminated." Killed by: the ideal-solver sanity check — a perfect reversal-tracker holds each action ~100 ticks and scores freeze_rate ≈ 0.99; both K6 candidates scored 0.95–0.99 while fully resolving the NR-A-004 harm (phase-2 'c' fraction 0.0 → 0.78–0.79, total 85 → 128). A metric a perfect solver fails is measuring the wrong thing. Superseded by phase2_c_fraction > 0.5. Receipt: `flesh-pits/prototypes/architecture-a/receipts/k6_stationarity_resolution.json`.
17. **Tracking-EMA reward baseline for attention gains (NR-A-001)** — Claim: per-channel gain delta rule with a tracking reward-EMA baseline learns relevance shifts. Killed by: fast EMA (alpha=0.05) → baseline tracked the poor post-reversal reward, error→0, gains froze, agent perseverated; slow EMA (alpha=0.01) → error shrank ~10x, switching needed > phase length. The baseline must not track the current policy's reward, or learning provably stalls. Fixed with a constant aspiration baseline (0.5); K4 then passes 4/4.
18. **All-history (Welford) surprise baseline for admission gating** — Claim: all-history mean+std over prediction error gates episodic admission. Killed by: build-time test — the initial learning transient dominates the statistics, so after ~10 steps almost nothing new ever exceeds mean+std (store stayed at 1–3 episodes after 80 steps). Replaced with an EMA (recency-weighted) baseline: surprise is relative to CURRENT expectations. (flesh-pits build-time negative.)
19. **Unobserved-arm punishment (NR-A-002)** — Claim: utility=0 for unacted channels is a neutral default. Killed by: unacted channels received negative updates (0 − baseline), gains crushed to floor, policy froze on the first winner permanently. Rules out negative credit assignment without observation. Fixed: update only channels present in the utility evidence.
20. **Sequential within-tick ignition carryover (NR-A-003)** — Claim: reverberant ignition strength = working-memory persistence. Killed by: sequential per-item ignition seeded each item from the previous item's settled strength — ignition outcomes depended on admission order (caught by the identity-symmetry check: trajectories not bit-identical). Rules out naive sequential reverberation. Fixed: common seed per tick (within-tick independence), dominant settled strength carries across ticks.
21. **The literal 0.5B↔1.5B transfer arm for the model-free K4 loop** — Claim (task framing): "train with model A (0.5B), transfer to model B (1.5B), measure the K4 win." Killed by: inspection (verified by own hand, documented in `experiments/transfer_k4_restore.py`'s header) — the K4 loop contains NO foundation model: specialists are pure functions, arbitrate()/update_gains() touch only floats, feedback utility is {action: reward}. Loading two models and never calling them, then reporting "behavior survives replacement," would be pretend machinery — refused under the nothing-ever-fake law and the Estate Formation Charter. Replaced by the honest variant: transfer ONLY the persisted gains JSON across a loop restart into a fresh loop (fresh z-score estimators, fresh ignition strength, fresh reward EMA, fresh RNG envs), freeze, and measure whether the K4 win survives (gates G1/G2/G3 in the script header; verdict pending with the other worker).
22. **D22 axiom donor (VersesTech/axiom)** — Claim: adopt the active-inference agent toolkit. Killed by: license audit — academic-only, revocable, non-sublicensable (a legal trap even under the owner's waived harvest-license gate; provenance blackout cannot be honored with a revocable license). The mechanism is covered by pymdp (donor D11, EXPERIMENT_AGAINST). Disposition: REJECT. Repo @ `4827bf427909f6c698` (2025-06-02).
23. **"Precision weighting needs only shift-robustness" (C2-fragile reading)** — Standing note: the Phase-5 replication made the C2 kill FRAGILE (3/5 — estimated precision wins 2 seeds by 0.8, 3.5). The engineering rejection stands (uniform wins more often and by larger margins; C2B independently rejects the shift-aware variant), but the original single-seed kill margin is seed-dependent — cite as KILL, FRAGILE, never as a clean kill. The idea is SHELVED: no further precision variants preregistered. (Extension of entry 3b, not a new mechanism.)
