## Cross-cutting findings (lab law now) — Phase 5 additions

The fragility is specific to B's adaptive machinery, not the reproduction method.

10. **The |e0| probe dilution lesson (K3):** a prediction-error probe dominated by unpredictable channels measures channel unpredictability, not hierarchy. Instruments must be channel-scoped before they can claim about structure.
