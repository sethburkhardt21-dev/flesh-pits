*Dark Lord's Estate · parallel research lane · standing directive: `PARALLEL_RESEARCH_DIRECTIVE.md` (kept in the Estate chamber, not published here)*

continual learning, memory, temporal continuity, self/world distinction,
metacognition, consciousness-relevant computation — without reproducing any
existing architecture. Competing architectures are designed, implemented,
benchmarked, lesioned, and ablated. What survives experiments gets harvested

identities, no forced persona. Workspace labels must not bias cognition.

**Consciousness: UNRESOLVED.** Never inferred from memory, recurrence,
self-report, or sophisticated behavior. Mechanisms are tested, not narratives.
